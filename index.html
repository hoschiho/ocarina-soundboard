Ocarina Songbook

Playing into the Switch 2 mic

  1. In the game, open + → System → Options and set Ocarina Mic Input to Instruments.
  2. Take out the ocarina and hold your phone near the console.
  3. Tap a song. Tap it again to stop.
  4. Not recognised? Try Speed: Slow or Tone: Pure, and check with Practice Mode (−).

No sound on iPhone? Turn off silent mode. Long-press the scarecrow to change your Scarecrow's Song.

Turn your phone sideways for the full songbook.
